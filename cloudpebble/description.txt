TITLE

ASCII Mood


TAGLINE

Express yourself in five characters.


DESCRIPTION

ASCII Mood puts a text emoticon on your wrist at full size — the face fills the display, and the clock steps back into a quiet line underneath it.

Eight moods are built in, from (^_^) to (o_O), (>_<), (;_;) and (-_-). Pick one on the settings page, or just shake your wrist to flick to the next one — no menus, no buttons, and it sticks until you change it again. A short buzz confirms the switch, and if you would rather keep one face for good, shake switching turns off.

The face is drawn in a proper typeface rather than the built-in system fonts, so it reads as a deliberate piece of lettering at 90 pt rather than oversized small print. Hours, colon and minutes each take their own colour, so the time can pick up an accent without competing with the face.

Nothing else is on the screen. No date, no steps, no battery bar — just a face and the time, on a background you choose.

Built for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2, with the face sized for each display. The clock follows whatever 12-hour or 24-hour setting your watch is already using.


SETTINGS

- Face — which of the eight moods to show. Shaking the watch moves to the next one from wherever you are.
- Shake to change mood — turn this off to keep the mood fixed to what you picked above.
- Background — the colour behind everything.
- Face — the colour of the emoticon.
- Hour digits, Colon, Minute digits — the three parts of the clock, coloured separately.
